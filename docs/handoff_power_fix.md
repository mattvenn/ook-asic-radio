### 2. RX supply: the real RX improvement
- Now: VGND avg_sc 67 Ω, comp_ct 57, r2r 35, bias 31; VDPWR log_det / avg_sc / comp_ct / dbg_tg
  27–32 Ω; flat to 500 MHz, so the decaps (behind it) do nothing at the blocks.
- Cause: **not the straps** (ideal straps leave it unchanged) **and not just the route width** (routing
  ×3 barely moves VGND): the RX ground current winds through other blocks' rails (bias met3 ~14 Ω,
  decap part 3 met1 ~10 Ω, the chain, comp_ct) and the 1 µm routes, ~150 Ω in series.
- **Change: dedicated VGND_RX and VDPWR_RX trunks, ≥ 4 µm, met3 + met4 stacked where free, from the
  mid straps (x ~278) straight to avg_sc, comp_ct, lpf, log_det, Ctrim / r2r and bias**, so no block's
  current runs through another block's rails. Trim decap parts 1, 3, 4 where the trunks need room
  (Matt agreed decap can go). Avoid met4 over MIM top plates; keep the lpf / avg / trim analog nodes'
  shielding intent from `docs/handoff_toplevel.md`.
- Expected (estimate, not simulated): RX R to roughly a quarter (avg_sc / comp_ct VGND ~15–20 Ω).

## Known limits of the power flow
- magic's extresist can't do supply nets (it lumps them into the port); the flow uses its own mesh
  of the traced metal, solved in ngspice. Details in docs/power.md "Method".
- The TT PDN outside the tile is modelled from tt-multiplexer (`sim/power/pdn.py`); the package
  (0.5 Ω, 2 nH, 1 nF chip C) is a guess. Substrate coupling isn't modelled.
- The 1 µm mesh (AC runs) reads RX ground ~30 % low; DC numbers use 0.5 µm.
