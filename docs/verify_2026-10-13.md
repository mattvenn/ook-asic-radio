[verification.md](verification.md), including the ones added in phases 1–3 (verify_supply_mc.md,

## Open items from this run

- **Finding #13 (new): overload recovery 0.25–0.3 ms** after a −10 dBm burst (was < 0.1 ms), from H135's
  balance point being further from where an overload pulls the servo. A strong nearby transmitter blinds the
  receiver for ~0.3 ms after it stops. Recorded, not fixed (a faster servo after a long one-sided run, or a
  reset code nearer 179, would shorten it; both are RTL changes).
- amp_dp / amp_dpc extracted netlists updated to magic 8.3.642 (−0.23 / −0.45 dB in their block tests); the
  block-vs-tile extraction disagreement (finding #5) is otherwise unchanged.
- Not run: the closed loop through the extracted RF chain (finding #8, ~10 000 s per µs).
- Scheduler lessons (in `sim/rerun/sched.py`): `tools/osic`'s exit status is unreliable here (judge by the
  output files); a short job that gates a long one needs a high priority; long jobs need reserved threads;
  scripts that rewrite a shared netlist at start need staggered starts, not locks.
