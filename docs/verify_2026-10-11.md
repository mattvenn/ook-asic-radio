- **magic 8.3.684 drops node-to-substrate C from `ext2spice`.** For example, tx_drv `out` drops from
  78 to 29 fF, comp_ct `inp` from 28 to 8.7 fF, and lpf_rc `out` loses all 25 fF. magic computes the C
  but doesn't write it. Every extracted result would look better than it is. All extractions in this
  run use magic 8.3.642, built locally (`~/.local/magic642`, `OSIC_MAGIC=<bin dir>`). The bad
  8.3.684 extractions are kept in `build/verify/pex_m684/`. DRC is the same on both (checked on
  decap_vapwr_p1).
- **KLayout 0.30.12:** the rebuilt tile differs from the committed `gds/` only by ≤5 nm slivers in
  lna_chain, comp_ct and bias_gen (rounding). LEF is identical. DRC is clean on both. The export was
  reverted.
- **ngspice 47:** small shifts. RX corner gains are within ±0.6 dB of the documented ranges. The −94 dBm
  mixed-signal run flips from detected to not detected (see below).
- **Wrappers:** inside the image there is no docker. `tools/osic` and `tools/osic-mac` now exec
  `tools/osic-inimage` when `$IIC_OSIC_TOOLS_VERSION` is set. `tools/longrun` falls back to `nohup`
  without systemd. 
- **sim-0 has an 8-CPU quota** (`/sys/fs/cgroup/cpu.max`), although `nproc` says 30. With 8 ngspice
  running, cgroup throttling cut throughput ~10×: the joined runs dropped from ~21 to ~2 µs/h. Keep it
  to **≤ 3 ngspice at once**.

## Joined RX (whole receiver at transistor level, RF noise)

### −90 dBm, schematic (new level)
`plot_joined.py` reported an on/off LPF swing of 0.63 mV against 1.80 mV from the model. Diagnosis:

1. **Window choice (the cause).** `plot_joined.py` skipped 40 µs after every window start, including
   the first off chip (20–60 µs → empty) and the last (372–400 µs → empty). That left the off level to
   one 64 µs window. Per chip the det swing is **1.81 mV** for chip 1 (model 1.80) and ~1.4 mV for
   chip 2, which sits on a slow +3 mV wander. With 10 µs blocks: 1.28 ± 0.48 mV (1σ). Fixed in
   `plot_joined.py` (df17a26): det now skips 2 µs after each edge, and the LPF settle applies only
   after key edges. It now reads 1.33 mV det swing.
2. **Noise bandwidth:** the chain's ENBW is 407–454 MHz (model 450). Not the cause.
3. **Noise-free CW through the real chain** (det drop vs model):

   | Level | real | model |
   |---|---|---|
   | −100 dBm | 0.55 mV | 0.77 mV |
   | −90 dBm | 5.42 mV | 6.27 mV |
   | −80 dBm | 43.5 mV | 47.3 mV |
   | −70 dBm | 177.0 mV | 175.0 mV |

   Square law holds. The real chain is ~0.6 dB less sensitive at low level. No deterministic drift
   (0.15 mV over 60 µs).
4. **Wander:** two noise-only runs (seeds 777 / 778, 200 ps noise step) give 10 µs det block sd 0.79 /
   0.88 mV and LPF sd 0.51 / 0.65 mV (model ~0.67 / 0.59). The joined run's off-chip sd is 1.37 mV
   (n = 14). Same order, so chip 2's wander is RF noise, not a defect.
5. **Device (flicker) noise**, ngspice `.noise` at det: 35 µV/√Hz at 10 Hz, 5.1 at 1 kHz, 1.9 at
   10 kHz. That is 0.45 mV rms after the LPF and 0.34 mV rms in LPF − average. The model budgets 0.3 mV
   comparator noise. e2e with 0.45 mV: −92 and −94 dBm still 5/5.

**Conclusion:** false alarm. The circuit matches the model within ~0.5 dB, and the e2e sensitivity
(−92…−93 dBm) stands. The committed `rx_joined.png` (−70 dBm) is unchanged.

### Joined RX on the extracted tile (new)
The full 400 µs run on the extracted tile (c mode) ran at ~2 µs/h, about 8 days in total, so it was
stopped. A short replacement was run: two 30 µs runs at **−92 dBm**, keyed on at 1 µs, one with the
signal and one without, with the same noise seed (now `sim/top/tb_tile_joined.py pair`). The on − off
difference in det / LPF shows whether the signal survives the extracted layout at the right size.
30 µs is under a third of one 104 µs chip, so this checks signal level, not detection; the
mixed-signal runs check detection. The comparator can't discriminate here: the LPF falls from 1.49 to
1.42 V as the RF noise floor pulls det down, and the 0.42 ms averager can't follow in 30 µs.

| window | det off − on | LPF off − on |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 µs | +0.02 mV | +0.11 mV |
| 5–10 µs | +2.37 mV | +0.44 mV |
| 10–20 µs | +0.86 mV | +1.14 mV |
| 20–30 µs | +0.14 mV | +0.90 mV |
| **2–30 µs mean** | **+0.84 mV** | |

**Result: ✅ consistent.** The model expects ~1.1 mV at −92 dBm (1.80 mV at −90 dBm, square law). The
shared seed removes the noise-only term, but the signal × noise cross term remains. (Correction,
2026-10-12: ngspice 47 doesn't repeat trnoise between runs, with `.options seed`, `set rndseed` or
`setseed`, so these two runs probably had independent noise and the ±0.5 mV is optimistic. The `pair`
mode now feeds both runs one noise file; rerun it for a clean number.) It wanders by a
few mV over 5–10 µs, as in the joined runs, so 28 µs gives about ±0.5 mV. The signal reaches det and
the LPF on the extracted tile with the expected sign and size. This is not a precise gain measurement.
The off run hit "timestep too small" at exactly 30 µs, after writing 3000 of 3001 points, so its data
is complete. Analysis: `tb_tile_joined.py --no-run pair -92` (the original `build/` scripts were lost with the pod; rebuilt 2026-10-12).

## Overload, −10 dBm (new)

The joined deck with the same keyed on/off/on stimulus at −10 dBm:
- The comparator follows the keying: rising at 60.34 / 269.29 µs, falling at 190.54 / 392.28 µs (26
  and 20 µs after key-off).
- det saturates at 0.563 V and recovers in ~10 µs. The LPF reaches 0.57 V and recovers in ~45 µs.
- The averager is pulled from 1.439 to 1.304 V over the run, ~70 mV per burst. With τ 0.42 ms it
  recovers in ~1.3 ms, so a weak burst right after a very strong one may need that long before the
  threshold is back (not simulated).
- No latch-up and no stuck state.
